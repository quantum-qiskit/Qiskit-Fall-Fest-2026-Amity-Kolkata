# Qiskit Fall Fest 2026 — Amity University Kolkata

**Qiskit Fall Fest** is a worldwide series of community-led quantum computing events organized on university campuses. This is the official event page for the **Amity University Kolkata** chapter.

---

## What is this?

A free, open event for students, researchers, and anyone curious about quantum computing — hosted by Amity University Kolkata in collaboration with **IBM Quantum**.

Attend workshops, hear from experts, get hands-on with real quantum hardware, and connect with the quantum community.
